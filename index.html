P-01 In use at 2 companies

Order Management System

One system for furniture exporters to run every order from the buyer's purchase order to the final container. Office, production and dispatch staff use it for up to 25 buyer POs a month, instead of tracking orders across Excel files, emails and messages.

Buyer PO Proforma invoice + carton & sticker orders Contractor POs 11 production stages Dispatch Remaining-to-dispatch report

Stages run Raw Taken, In Process, Raw Ready, Fumigation, Sanding, Potein, Color, Katai & Coloring, Polish, Packing and Final Dispatch.

  • Type a product code and quantity; description, size, CBM and rates fill in from the product master, with live totals.
  • Saving an order generates the proforma invoice, carton order and sticker order as PDFs.
  • Split one item across several contractors, with auto-numbered contractor POs in the company's own format.
  • Phone-friendly Shop Floor page for supervisors to update production stages.
  • Reports on screen, in Excel or PDF, filtered by buyer, PO, product code, date and contractor.
  • Separate logins for admin, office, production and dispatch; prices are removed on the server for roles that may not see them, not just hidden in the browser.
  • Challenge solved: one order line can be split across several contractors while the system keeps track of the quantity still unassigned.

P-02 In use at 3 companies

Product Management and Catalogue System

A product library for companies with large ranges, now holding 500+ products: every product gets a QR code and barcode, a scan opens its details, and buyer catalogues are generated from a selection.

Excel import QR code + barcode Printed labels Scan Protected costing Buyer selection Catalogue
  • Excel upload with automatic column mapping, duplicate handling and bulk updates; bulk images matched by file name.
  • Scanning a product's QR code opens a mobile page that shows only the fields chosen for buyers.
  • Costing stays behind a PIN, sign-in and role permission, and every view is logged.
  • Real-time costing: change the USD rate, wastage, freight or margins and the price recalculates instantly.
  • Catalogue generator with simple, premium and buyer-specific templates, exported to PDF, PowerPoint or Excel.
  • Labels in bulk on A4 sheets, thermal sizes or 10 × 10 cm stickers; field-level change history for every product.
  • Challenge solved: imports each company's existing costing workbook and checks every calculated price against the sheet.
  • An automated end-to-end test with 142 checks re-runs the main workflows on a throwaway database with one command.
